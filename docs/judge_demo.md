# Judge Demo Script

**Project:** SurakshaPath AI  
**Tagline:** Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla  
**Hackathon:** BGI Hackathon 2026  
**Theme:** Smart Cities & Urban Innovation

## 3-Minute Pitch Flow

### 0:00 to 0:20 - Problem

"In smart cities, people usually optimize for the fastest route, not the safest one. Current maps do not explain safety risk, do not react to live incidents well, and do not help city admins see where danger is increasing."

### 0:20 to 0:50 - Solution

"SurakshaPath AI is a safety-first navigation platform that predicts safer routes, explains why they are safer, streams live alerts, and gives admins a dashboard for urban safety monitoring."

### 0:50 to 1:40 - Live Demo Steps

1. Open the home screen and point out the branding and splash experience.
2. Enter a source and destination.
3. Click **Find Safe Route**.
4. Show the route comparison cards and explain the AI safety score.
5. Switch to **Live Navigation** and start the route.
6. Trigger **Demo Simulation** to inject a live incident.
7. Show the smart alert toast, reroute recommendation, and live timeline update.
8. Open the admin dashboard to show analytics and recent incidents.

### 1:40 to 2:20 - AI Explanation

"The model uses a Random Forest engine trained on a 50,000-row dataset. It returns a safety score, classification label, confidence, risk breakdown, and explainable factors so users understand why the route is recommended."

### 2:20 to 2:45 - Impact

"This platform can help citizens make safer travel decisions, help authorities understand safety hotspots, and support real-time incident awareness in urban areas."

### 2:45 to 3:00 - Future Scope

"Future versions can add an IoT panic button, real police API integration, real-time CCTV analytics, and mobile deployment for broader public adoption."

---

## Judge Delivery Tips

- Speak clearly and move from problem to proof quickly.
- Keep the demo route simple and reproducible.
- Emphasize that the system is explainable, not just predictive.
- Mention that the stack is production-shaped and portfolio-ready.
- Highlight MySQL only, OpenStreetMap only, and Random Forest only for architecture clarity.
