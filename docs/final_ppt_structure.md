**Project:** SurakshaPath AI  
**Tagline:** Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla  
**Hackathon:** BGI Hackathon 2026

## Recommended 10-12 Slide Structure

| Slide | Title | What to Show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title Slide | Project name, tagline, team name, hackathon branding |
| 2 | Problem | Unsafe travel, poor route awareness, delayed incident response |
| 3 | Solution | AI safety navigation platform overview |
| 4 | AI Architecture | Frontend, FastAPI backend, Random Forest engine, MySQL, OpenStreetMap, OSRM, WebSocket layer |
| 5 | Tech Stack | Key technologies with icons and one-line responsibilities |
| 6 | ML Model | Random Forest, 50,000-row dataset, 99.65% accuracy, explainable output |
| 7 | Demo Flow | Route search, comparison, live navigation, demo simulation |
| 8 | Live Navigation | ETA, progress, reroute recommendation, alert feed |
| 9 | Heatmap | Unsafe zones, incident clusters, safety layers |
| 10 | Admin Dashboard | Metrics, recent incidents, heatmap summary, insights |
| 11 | Impact | Citizen safety, urban visibility, operational value |
| 12 | Future Scope | IoT panic button, police integration, CCTV analytics, mobile app |

## Suggested Presentation Flow

### Slide 1: Title

Start with a bold hero slide and the tagline.

### Slide 2: Problem

Explain why normal maps are not enough for safety-first navigation.

### Slide 3: Solution

Position SurakshaPath AI as an explainable smart-city safety product.

### Slide 4: Architecture

Show the full system flow from route input to AI output and live updates.

### Slide 5: Tech Stack

Keep this fast and visual.

### Slide 6: ML Model

Explain the Random Forest model in simple language.

### Slide 7: Live Demo

Show the route comparison and live navigation path.

### Slide 8: Heatmap

Show unsafe zones and incident density.

### Slide 9: Admin Dashboard

Demonstrate operational usefulness for city stakeholders.

### Slide 10: Impact

End with why the product matters in the real world.

### Slide 11 or 12: Future Scope

Leave judges with a strong roadmap and product vision.

## Design Tips

- Use a dark futuristic theme with cyan, emerald, and blue accents.
- Keep each slide visually clean and not text-heavy.
- Prefer diagrams, icons, metric cards, and screenshots over paragraphs.
- Show live product UI wherever possible.
- Keep one message per slide.
