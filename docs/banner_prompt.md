# README Hero Banner Prompt

**Project:** SurakshaPath AI  
**Tagline:** Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla  
**Hackathon:** BGI Hackathon 2026

## AI Image Prompt

Create a premium **futuristic smart-city hero banner** for a safety navigation platform named **SurakshaPath AI**. The scene should feel like a **modern startup landing page** mixed with a **high-end sci-fi city control room**.

### Visual Direction

- A glowing night-time **Indian smart city** inspired by **Indore**
- Clear road networks visualized as illuminated route lines
- AI navigation overlays floating above the streets
- Safety heatmaps with layered neon gradients showing safe and unsafe zones
- A strong **women safety focus** represented through protective route guidance and safer corridor highlights
- Smart routing visualization with arrows, waypoints, and route comparison panels
- An elegant cyber aesthetic with teal, cyan, emerald, and electric blue accents
- Subtle magenta and violet highlights for contrast
- A polished startup product feel, not a game screenshot

### Scene Elements

- A futuristic city skyline with roads, traffic hints, and civic infrastructure
- A map-like ground plane with route paths glowing across the city
- Heatmap overlays around incident-prone zones
- Smart AI nodes connecting to route checkpoints
- A central UI-style composition with navigation panels and safety score badges
- A sense of motion, intelligence, and public trust

### Mood and Tone

- Confident
- Safe
- Innovative
- Urban
- Highly professional
- Judge-ready

### Composition Guidance

- Wide banner composition for README header use
- Clean negative space for title placement
- Soft depth of field with cinematic lighting
- Balanced symmetry with a strong central route path
- No clutter, no chaotic traffic, no dystopian destruction

### Suggested Text Overlay

- **SurakshaPath AI**
- **Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla**
- **AI-powered safe route prediction for smart cities**

### Style Keywords

Modern startup, futuristic UI, neon smart city, safe navigation, route intelligence, urban innovation, heatmap visualization, explainable AI, premium product banner, cinematic lighting, polished hackathon branding.

### Negative Prompt

Avoid low-resolution visuals, cartoon style, messy composition, horror atmosphere, excessive cyberpunk grit, unsafe street chaos, random typography, fake logos, and overly dark unreadable scenes.
