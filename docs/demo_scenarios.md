# Demo Safety Data Scenarios

**Project:** SurakshaPath AI  
**Tagline:** Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla  
**Hackathon:** BGI Hackathon 2026

## Scenario 1: Woman Travelling at Night

### Story

A woman is traveling alone after dark from a residential area to a market area. The route has low lighting, limited CCTV coverage, and a few recent incident reports.

### Demo Goal

Show how SurakshaPath AI highlights the safer alternative, explains the risk, and triggers smart alerts when the route becomes less safe.

### What to Emphasize

- women safety risk
- lighting quality
- police station proximity
- live reroute recommendation

### Judge Impact

This scenario connects emotionally because it addresses a very real urban safety concern.

---

## Scenario 2: Student Late-Evening Travel

### Story

A student is returning home after classes at dusk. The fastest route is shorter, but the AI flags a safer alternative because of incident density and weak street lighting.

### Demo Goal

Show that the system balances travel convenience with personal safety.

### What to Emphasize

- time of day
- street light coverage
- incident count
- route comparison explanation

### Judge Impact

This scenario is easy to understand and shows how the product helps daily commuters.

---

## Scenario 3: Unsafe Rerouting Scenario

### Story

The journey starts on a safe route, but a new incident appears near the corridor during live navigation. The system detects the change and recommends a safer reroute.

### Demo Goal

Prove that the platform is live and adaptive, not static.

### What to Emphasize

- live incident injection
- AI alerts
- reroute logic
- navigation timeline update

### Judge Impact

This is the most powerful demo scenario because it shows real-time intelligence and product maturity.

---

## Demo Presentation Tip

Use scenario 3 during the final demo if you want the strongest visual and emotional response from judges.
