# Final Pitch Script

**Project:** SurakshaPath AI  
**Tagline:** Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla  
**Hackathon:** BGI Hackathon 2026

## 3-Minute Speaking Script

"Good morning judges.

Imagine you are leaving home late at night. Your map app tells you the fastest route, but not the safest one. That is the problem SurakshaPath AI solves.

SurakshaPath AI is a smart-city safety navigation platform built for safer travel decisions. It predicts route safety, explains why a route is safe or unsafe, shows heatmaps of risky areas, and gives live alerts when conditions change.

Our solution has three layers. First, the frontend gives a clean smart-city experience with route comparison, live navigation, and incident reporting. Second, the FastAPI backend manages predictions, analytics, and real-time safety data. Third, a Random Forest model scores routes using a dataset of safety signals and returns an explainable safety decision, not just a number.

Let me show the demo. I search for a route, compare the safer option, and start live navigation. During the demo simulation, the system injects a new incident, sends AI alerts, and recommends a safer reroute. On the admin side, we can see incidents, metrics, and heatmap patterns that help city teams understand where risk is growing.

The impact is simple: citizens can make smarter travel decisions, and city operators can monitor urban safety more clearly. This is why we built SurakshaPath AI: to turn safety data into a confident action.

Our tagline says it best: Surakshit Raasta, Smart Faisla. Thank you."

## Delivery Notes

- Speak slowly in the first 20 seconds.
- Emphasize the words "safer route" and "explainable AI."
- Point to the screen during the demo instead of over-explaining.
- Keep transitions smooth and confident.
- End with the tagline to make it memorable.
